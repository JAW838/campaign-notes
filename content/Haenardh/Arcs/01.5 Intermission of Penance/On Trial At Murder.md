# Before the Trial
The Seven confront the [[Tech Hunters]] in their base at the [[Batel#The Big Tower|Big Tower]] during the [[Siege on The Big Tower]].
# On Trial At Murder
- [[2026-01-03]]: The [[Roving Seven]] land on the [[Valorya#Disdain|Island of Disdain]]. [[Reen Voidswift]], who is wanted by the [[Blood Hunters]], is offered redemption on the condition that she slay Boreas, a monster on the neighboring [[Valorya#Cruelty|Island of Cruelty]]. When the rest of the Seven decide to go with her, they board a boat and make their way to the island. They briefly ally with a certain [[Lenchtahg|regenerating fiend]] but quickly betray it, keeping its skull in a backpack. Night falls and they take shelter in a cave but are attacked by a group of wolf-like creates, and while most are killed Reen is dragged away.
- [[2026-01-17]]: The Seven follow the creatures to [[Valorya#The Hollow|The Hollow]] where they find Boreas and Reen. As the fight devolved into a struggle for survival, [[Leon Redbrand|Leon]] cashed in a favour, summoning a black tendril monster from a coin in his pocket to rescue the party. After a hard-fought victory, a surprised ferryman escorted them back to Cruelty.
- [[2026-01-31]]: Reen is accepted back into the Hunters and a party ensues. The next day the Seven take off for [[The Nimyren Empire#Narmor|Narmor]] in their loaned airship, and when the fiend begins regenerating they throw it overboard. In the capital city, they meet with several dignitaries including [[Emperor Ballas]] himself, receive pardons, and sign a [[2026-01-31#Contract|contract with the government]] to eradicate the remaining [[Tech Hunters]] and retrieve their research.
# Post-Trial Hunt
The time has come. The [[Tech Hunters]] have made their last enemy, and the Seven go to [[The Scarlet Sands]] to finish them off in [[The Hunters Become the Prey]].
