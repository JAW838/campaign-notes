> _“The world is full of whimsy.”_
# Notes
- The [[Roving Seven]] continue on their journey to confront [[Gestalt]]. As they reach his lair, they discover a sign on the door reading "Welcome Roving Idiots". [[Ondo Edafoss|Ondo]] wonders which of the party members this flirting message is directed towards. They cautiously enter.
- Loose notes, surgical tools, and various body parts lay strewn around the room, covering the tables, chairs, and floor. Large drains in the floor hide the blood of countless victims, and the air reeks of suffering and pain. Behind closed doors lie incomprehensible green monsters, writhing masses of tentacles, things too terrible to say here.
- 2 wolf-like creatures with bald cat-like heads and snakes coming from their necks arrive to escort the [[Roving Seven|Seven]] to meet [[Gestalt]] deeper in the base.
- They arrive in a large laboratory, with a large central area for experiments, tesla coils at around the edge, and an elevated platform at the back. Gestalt stands on the platform with an oversized blue Slaad in a stockade behind him. The level below is crawling with the wolf-creatures and other modified Slaads.
- During his monologue, [[Gestalt]] remarks that he is not privy to [[Gideon McEiben|Gideon]]'s plans, and that he is here only for the funding and experiments. He admires Gideon and [[Paraceleus]] for their intellect.
- The fighting begins as [[Gideon McEiben|Gideon]] attempts to revive his win condition, a dead thessylbeholder in the centre of the room. As the tesla coils begin to crackle with energy, the party fights desperately to stop them and keep the creature dead.
- [[Richard]] uses his spear of rusting to rust a huge chunk of a coil, playing a key piece in the party's successful bid to prevent the thessylbeholder's resurrection.
- Taking heavy damage, [[Gestalt]] teleports to the far side of the upper platform. He attempts to cast Globe of Invulnerability but is stopped by [[Corvel]]'s Counterspell. [[Leon Redbrand]] then teleports after him and removes his head.
- With his death the doors in the previous room open, releasing a Nullifier, Reverser and Absorber which enter the fray. Because it was fed by a party member earlier, the Absorber fights alongside the party and plays a key role in killing the Reverser.
- At the same time, [[Kajal]] bursts through the back wall of the upper platform and grabs [[Gestalt]]'s decapitated head. He arrives with [[Kajal#Allies|Bobo the Bonobo and Maurice the Orangatang]], who enter the fray on the lower level to loot valuable items.
- [[Leon Redbrand|Leon]] attempts to touch [[Kajal]]'s hat, which triggers him to punch Leon so hard he loses consciousness, at which point he loses control and becomes a writhing mass of flame, going berserk on Kajal.
- After a difficult fight, which would have been much harder with the revival of the thessylbeholder and had the Absorber not joined them, the Seven are victorious. [[Gestalt]] is dead, and many of his most powerful creations are as well.
- Having been attacked by [[Ondo Edafoss|Ondo]] and with more of the Seven on their way, [[Kajal]] recalls his allies and they are lifted into the air by the [[Kajal#Kajal-Copter|Kajal-Copter]].
- With the fight over, the Seven retreat and take a long rest in a new safe house.
