> _“Tidally locked Ondo.”_
# Notes
- The [[Roving Seven]] splits into 2 groups in hopes of finding a Clockwork Horror, which looks like a spider robot. [[Corvel]] and [[Leif Vornsen|Leif]] find nothing but [[Ondo Edafoss|Ondo]] and [[Reen Voidswift|Reen]] find one. 
- Ondo ground pounds it for massive damage. Reen casts fireball which destroys it but makes quite a commotion, drawing attention from nearby enemies. In an attempt to escape Reen casts Dimension Door and the two are transported into a doll house.
- Corvel and Leif arrive, trapping the pursuing enemies in a cone of ice.
- Fastforwarding to when the Seven have regrouped at the safehouse, Reen and Corvel work together to extract a magic-extracting device from the Clockwork Horror. This item resembles a rudimentary version of the [[The Talisman|Talisman]] from Leif's backstory. The following items are sacrificed to the object:
1. a longsword of showmanship (common),
2. a set of glamoured studded leather (rare),
3. potion of thunder resistance (uncommon), and
4. arby's gift card (rare)
- The resultant liquid magic is used to construct 5 magical bombs. A small amount of the liquid is also placed in a pan and set alight. A green smoke emanates from the burning magic as abstract shapes swirl within it, and the smoke knocks Ondo down.
- Against all better judgement, Ondo drinks 4 ounces of the magical liquid, impermanently gaining a 60ft fly speed after wings painfully burst from his back. Leif casts invisibility on him and he goes scouting, bringing back a stained glass painting he expertly cut out with his fingernail.
- With their newly crafted explosive devices the party moves into the sewers to advance their plan.
- As they make their way through the sewers, Leif notices a Clockwork Horror approaching the party from behind. He telepathically warns the rest of the party, and after a countdown they turn in unison and deal enough damage to destroy it before it has a chance to react.
- However all the magic used in this attack draws attention, and the party is forced into combat by approaching enemies.
- After a quick fight, the Seven proceed through the sewers, enduring high temperatures spewed by various mechanisms lining the walls. They discover an unfinished subway system which takes them to the inner city, and as they travel through this system they enter a large room, with [[Theofrastus Bombastus]] standing at the other end.
- From the sky falls a mech and Bombastus climbs in.
#  Goals
- Complete [[Ondo Edafoss|Ondo]]'s air trial at the [[Sea of Storm]].
- Hunt down and destroy the remaining members of the [[Tech Hunters]].
