A long time ago (thousands of years), the creators of the city decided to make a god. didn't do it right, didn't have the technology to make it work but they tried. The "god" was something constructed to help build and maintain the city. It is not a person but an artificial thing. they were able to keep it online to an extent during [[The Shattering]]. after some time, survivors recognised its wisdom and age worshiped it. They invented new power sources to feed it including the Quintessense Arrays and items like [[The Talisman]] to feed it. After an amount of time, an accident occurred and the magic was extracted from every being in the city, rendering it dead. They are trying to revive the god and they are feeding it people/souls. Quintessence arrays were [[Gideon McEiben]]'s attempts to feed it. [[The Talisman]] can separate the arcane energy from an object (like removing a soul from a body).
