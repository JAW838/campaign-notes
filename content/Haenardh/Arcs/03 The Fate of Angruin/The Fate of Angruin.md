# Before the Wheel of Iron
[[The Hunters Become the Prey]] cover the Seven's journey across [[The Scarlet Sands]] in their search for the [[Tech Hunters]] base of operations.
# The Fate of Angruin
- [[2026-05-02]]: Their first target is [[Dee]]. As the [[Roving Seven]] move through the city, they come across a typewriter-like machine: a [[Mark 5 Analytical Engine]]. It can answer basic questions and was used by its owner to track shop finances and records. To find more information the party travels further into the city to the Analytical Archive, essentially the original Analytical Engine, and ask it a bunch of questions. They also find evidence of the original population of the city, frozen in time and fear and turned to metal.
- [[2026-06-13]]: After working together to enter [[Dee]]'s clock tower, the party climb through the building and engage them in battle. After a tough fight [[Dee]] is defeated, and the party flees before they are swarmed by enemies.
- [[2026-06-20]]: [[Leon Redbrand|Leon]] returns after a multi-session scouting mission with dire news: the [[The Scarlet Sands#Aetherforge|Aetherforge]] is operational. In order to shut it down, it must be deprived of energy. To that end, the party decides to attack one of the 9 power generation stations lining the outer edge of the city. On their way to the nearest station, the [[Roving Seven]] have their first official meeting with [[Ikol]], an ancient and mysterious being inhabiting a coin possessed by [[Leon Redbrand|Leon]]. After this, they did some recon and formed a plan: deprive the Hellfire engines of water and close inflow and outtake valves, starving the engine and shutting it down.
- [[2026-06-27]]: The fight begins. [[Roving Seven]] attack a generator station, destroying the power plant and the Immolith powering it. [[Theofrastus Bombastus]] was in the building but escaped. After barely surviving the fight, the group retreated to a new hideout to recover before taking the fight to [[Paraceleus]].
- [[2026-07-18]]: The party is welcomed by [[Paraceleus]] into the Aetherforge, a magical factory. A fight breaks out and [[Paraceleus]] escapes with Dimension Door. After the fight, the party experiment with the magical imbuement capabilities of the factory, including enchanting [[Ondo Edafoss|Ondo]]'s chair and [[Leif Vornsen]] himself.
- [[2026-08-01]]: [[Gestalt]] welcomes the Seven to his lair. He attacks the them with his thessyl-creatures but they are unable to protect him. When he realised he was losing he attempted to run away but could not stop [[Leon Redbrand|Leon]] from removing his head. At the same time, [[Kajal]] arrives with a group of cronies to ransack the place, escaping unscathed.
- [[2026-08-08]]: The [[Roving Seven|Seven]] recuperate from their recent ordeals. They determine the current location of the city and that [[Paraceleus]] and [[Theofrastus Bombastus]] are together in the central factory. A fight there may well include [[Gideon McEiben]]. A plan is concocted to get to the factory.
- [[2026-08-22]]: The party splits to capture a piece of enemy technology from a Clockwork Horror, allowing them to extract the magical energy from items. This energy is used to build bombs (and feed Ondo). They move through the sewers towards the factory, fighting a group of enemies along the way. Eventually they come across a large room with Theofrastus on the other side, at which point he climbs into a nearby mech suit.
