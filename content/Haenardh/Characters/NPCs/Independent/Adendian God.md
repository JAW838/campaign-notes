A synthetic being constructed to assist with all aspects of the city of [[Flying Cities#Adendia|Adendia]], the Adendian God's story culminates in a plot by the [[Tech Hunters]] to use its technology to kill countless people.
# Creation
Building a flying city is no easy feat, even with the help of a skilled inventor like [[The Architect]]. Built to assist in the design and maintenance of the new flying city, the Adendian God was like nothing else before it. Unparalleled intelligence and wisdom made it supremely effective at its given role, and its aid improved every aspect of the city and the teams building it. After the launch of [[Flying Cities#Adendia|Adendia]] its presence there was invaluable, solving countless problems and lubricating the wheels of progress.
# Death of a Civilisation
[[The Shattering]] affected Adendia in a unique way; because it was not suspended purely through magical means, it and its inhabitants largely survived their crash landing. This includes the God, who was, to an extent, kept online during and after the catastrophe. With their civilisation rapidly deteriorating many citizens turned to worshipping the God as a true God, using technology like Quintessense Arrays and items like [[The Talisman]] to feed it. But fate is a cruel mistress, and using machines to extract the magical energy from living beings never ends well. Indeed, sometime after the shattering an accident with one of these devices extracted the magical energy from every living thing in the city, rendering it completely lifeless and leaving nothing but metal corpses in every shop and street corner.
# Attempted Revival
At some point the [[Tech Hunters]] occupied the city and began their attempts to resurrect the Adendian God. These efforts included [[Gideon McEiben]] using the Quintessense Arrays along with newly developed machines far more effective than the [[The Talisman|Talisman]] they were based on.
