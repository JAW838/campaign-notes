# Notes 
- The [[Roving Seven]] heads towards the Aetherforge. The area is mostly devoid of guards and they successfully sneak through it without issue. Pipes and tubes filled with strange fluid flow deeper into the factory alongside belts carrying strange objects.
- A threatening voice rings out from the darkness, inviting the party to move deeper into the factory. They arrive in the large central chamber of the factory where they find [[Paraceleus]] at the end of a raised walkway. Along the walkway are four evenly spaced arches which produce a barrier preventing passage through them. 25 feet below the walkway is a river of the blue fluid - processed pure magical essence - from the pipes, and 35 feet from the side of the walkway is four railgun emplacements on ledges, two on each side.
- The river of magic and conveyer belts flow beneath a cog-shaped platform. On the platform there are tools to create magical items.
- The railguns are comprised mainly of a tube with crystals arranged inside and around them.
- After a tense exchange between [[Paraceleus]] and [[Corvel]], the fighting begins.
- A brutal battle ends with [[Paraceleus]] holding a lever and offering an opportunity: work together to take down [[Gestalt]], or lose everything now. The party agrees to end things now, so [[Paraceleus]] pulls the lever, draining the magic from the river below, and uses dimension door to escape with 3hp left. [[Corvel]] attempts to counterspell but [[Paraceleus]] uses a counterspell of his own to negate it.
- After the fight, [[Leif Vornsen]] rushes to return the lever to its upright state, allowing the party a few minutes to experiment with the enchantment setup.
- [[Ondo Edafoss]] attempts to imbue his plastic lawn chair with magic. It becomes a strange twisted fractal of what was once a lawn chair. [[Ondo Edafoss]] grieves its loss.
- [[Reen Voidswift|Reen]] and [[Leif Vornsen]] attempt to enchant a magical item and roll so well it releases a huge amount of magical energy in an explosion-like phenomenon. The object is transformed into an iridescent formation of crystals jutting out in all directions roughly 4 inches in diameter.
- [[Corvel]] offers a pair of manacles (handcuffs). After enchanting, they reach out to those who approach them and cuff them. Even after cuffing someone, they continue reaching out, chaining anyone nearby together. The "Chain Entity" is wrangled into a Bag of Holding.
- For the final experiment, [[Leif Vornsen]] offers himself to enchant. At the beginning he is levitated, then his back (with the runes) begins to sear in pain. He thrashes about as red and purple energy and dark smoke fill the room. Bits of his flesh stretch and contort. He becomes faster, his scales harden, his eyesight improves, and he gains a breath weapon as some draconic aspects of his genome are brought forth by the infusion of magic.
- After exhausting the remaining magical essence, the [[Roving Seven]] retreat and take a long rest.
